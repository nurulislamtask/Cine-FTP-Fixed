CineFTP — PC app (VLC diye cholbe)
===================================
1. Ei folder ta zip theke Extract All kore ber koro (zip er vitor theke chalabe na)
2. Start-CineFTP-PC.bat double-click koro — app er window nijei khule jabe
   (na khulle browser e jao: http://localhost:3000)
3. Movie khule player er upore "Play in VLC" button ta chaplei
   PC te VLC te movie cholbe — MKV shoho sob format cholbe.
   (VLC na thakle age videolan.org theke free install kore nio)
4. Phone theke dekhte chaile (ekoi WiFi): http://192.168.0.220:3000
   (tomar PC er IP alada hole bat window te lekha thakbe)
5. Server er window ta khola rakhba — bondho korle app o bondho.

Website version
===============
