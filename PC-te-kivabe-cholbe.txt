CineFTP — PC version
====================
1. Ei folder ta zip theke Extract All kore ber koro (zip er vitor theke chalabe na)
2. Start-CineFTP-PC.bat double-click koro, window ta khola rakho
3. Browser e jao: http://localhost:3000
4. Phone theke dekhte chaile (ekoi WiFi): http://192.168.0.220:3000
   (tomar PC er IP alada hole bat window tei lekha thakbe)
